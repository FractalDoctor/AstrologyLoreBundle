Astrology and Lore Suite

The astrology and folklore library for iPhone.

Seven reference apps for the night sky, the turning year and the old lore, built for pagan, Wiccan and esoteric practice. Every calculation runs on the device and none of them needs an account. All seven are one-off purchases with no subscriptions. Buy the suite and save compared to purchasing individually.

Included Apps

Wiccan Moon

Lunar phases through seven moon-naming traditions, from Celtic and Wiccan to Algonquin and English Medieval. Moon names and meanings, ritual guidance, spell timing by phase and sign, and a home screen widget.

More about Wiccan Moon

Wiccan Wheel of the Year

The eight sabbats, the solstices and equinoxes plus the four Celtic fire festivals, computed as real astronomical instants for your year, location and hemisphere rather than fixed calendar dates. Lore and correspondences for every festival.

More about Wiccan Wheel of the Year

Mansions of the Moon

The 28 lunar mansions of Agrippa and Barrett's celestial magic. The Moon's real-time ecliptic longitude gives the current mansion, with its ruling angel and traditional influences.

More about Mansions of the Moon

Celtic Tree Calendar

All 13 tree-signs of the Beth-Luis-Nion calendar with ogham letters, mythology, folklore, wood-craft uses and birth readings. Sourced lore, with the Robert Graves provenance made clear.

More about Celtic Tree Calendar

Zodiac Wheel

A live zodiac wheel with a hand at the true position of the Sun, a shuttle that runs the whole sky forwards and backwards, and complete natal birth charts in Whole Sign, Placidus or Equal houses. Every position, aspect and retrograde computed on the device.

More about Zodiac Wheel

Superstitions

More than 2,800 traditional beliefs, each read against a named historical source, chosen for the day you are in by the date, the feast, the moon, an eclipse or a meteor shower. Occasions finds the days tradition favours for marrying, travelling and planting.

More about Superstitions

Birthstones Wheel

38 traditions of birthstones by month, sign, weekday, hour, season and planet, from the breastplate of Aaron to the jewellers' list of 1912, read from Kunz's The Curious Lore of Precious Stones and shown side by side. Birth profiles gather every stone that applies to one person.

More about Birthstones Wheel
